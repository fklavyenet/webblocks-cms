# Code of Conduct

Contributors must participate respectfully and help maintain a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, avoid personal attacks and discriminatory or sexualized language, and never publish another person's private information without permission.

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Report conduct concerns privately through [fklavye.net](https://fklavye.net). Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

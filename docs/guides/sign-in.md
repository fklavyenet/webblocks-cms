# Sign In To The Admin Panel

**Goal:** Reach the WebBlocks CMS admin panel and understand what you are looking at.
**Time:** 1 minute
**You need:** Your site address and an account created for you by your site administrator

## Steps

1. Open your site address followed by `/webadmin`. For the example site in these guides that is `atlas-studio.test/webadmin`.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**, then select **Continue**.

> **Screenshot** `01-login.png` — The admin sign-in screen with the email address filled in.
> Alt: WebBlocks CMS admin sign-in screen.

3. You land on the dashboard. The left sidebar is your main navigation, and **Quick actions** puts the common tasks one click away.

> **Screenshot** `02-dashboard.png` — Dashboard with the sidebar and the Overview panel.
> Alt: WebBlocks CMS dashboard showing the sidebar and an overview of pages, blocks, and media.

## What Is Where

The four areas you will use most in this series:

- **Pages** — every page on the site, and where you create new ones.
- **Media** — images, videos, and documents you upload once and reuse.
- **Navigation** — the menus visitors click.
- **Shared Slots** — areas such as the header and footer, built once and reused on many pages.

**System** and **Maintenance** are for site setup and operations. You do not need them to write content.

## Notes

- If the sidebar shows fewer items than a colleague's, that is your role, not a fault. An **editor** sees content areas; a **site admin** also sees site settings; a **super admin** sees the whole installation, including Sites and System.
- Working on an installation with more than one site? Check which site is selected before you create anything. Pages belong to one site.
- To change the admin panel's language, use the language button in the top bar. It changes the admin interface, not your site content.
